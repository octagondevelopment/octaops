# Ideas (out of scope for Stage 2)

Logged here so they are not lost. Do not build these in the current phase.

- **WiFi-based check-in/check-out** — needs a native wrapper (Capacitor).
- **Automatic dispatch email parsing** — future: dedicated dispatch mailbox plus a webhook, instead of forwarding from a personal inbox.
- **QuickBooks integration**
- **Vixxo integration**
- **Multi-truck / warehouse stock management**
- **Push notifications** — evaluate after the PWA baseline works.
- **Report quality check** — flag weak technician reports automatically, using good and bad examples.
- **Offline clocking** — queue clock in and out on the phone and reconcile later. Skipped on purpose: the server sets the time so it cannot be changed.
- **Overtime shift tag** — a switch at clock-in for after-hours call-outs approved separately, if overtime ever needs a second source besides the automatic rules.
- **Payroll rules beyond the basics** — statutory holidays, averaging agreements, exempt roles.
- **Timesheet export** — CSV of the week for payroll.
